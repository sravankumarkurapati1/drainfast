# DrainFast
DrainFast predicts which Boston catch basins to clear before a storm and triages
storm-related flood reports during it.

Users: BWSC crew supervisors and Boston 311 staff.

Rules:
- Scope is storm-related reports only (flooding, catch basins, storm drains).
  Never add general 311 features (potholes, noise, trash).
- Staff approve every work order. Nothing is sent automatically.
- Every recommendation cites at least one past 311 ticket ID as evidence.
- Use plain language in all user-facing text.
